# SourceBound assistant PRD — revised

[R1] The system must accept plain-text uploads no larger than 1,000,000 bytes.
[R2] Every answer must be grounded in an uploaded source.
[R3] The assistant must refuse or request clarification when no uploaded source supports an answer.
[R4] Administrators may delete uploaded documents.
[R5] Regular members must not delete uploaded documents.
[R6] Every deletion decision must be recorded for manual policy review.
